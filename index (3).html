Founder's Story

Marut Nandan's Track Vision

"The concept of Pratibha.ai was born directly on the university athletic track. Balancing my academics in business analytics with competitive javelin, shot put, and long jump, I identified a massive gap in the Indian sports ecosystem.

Brilliant athletes in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities lacked objective performance metrics to optimize their technique and the visibility required to secure sponsorships for basic gear like lifting belts or proper shoes. By merging data science with computer vision, we turn standard smartphone videos into professional-grade analytics and democratize athlete funding."

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

Proprietary AI optimized for low-bandwidth. Direct sports + fintech marketplace. High social impact model.

Weaknesses

High initial R&D for video models. Reliance on user-generated camera qualities.

Opportunities

Integration with TOPS/Khelo India. Rise of Indian D2C brands. Deep mobile internet penetration.

Threats

Established analytics giants (Hudl) adapting. Sudden regulatory updates (DPDP Act compliance).
